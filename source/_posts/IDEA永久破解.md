# IDEA永久破解
> 获取临时激活码：https://www.idejihuo.cn/
![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/hfshaobing/picx-images-hosting@master/20251221/image.266lzskryegw.webp)

## 步骤

参考地址：https://blog.idejihuo.com/jetbrains/intellij-idea-2025-2-latest-activation-tutorial-permanent-activation-code-cracking-tool-2099.html

激活工具已保存至百度云盘，名称：jetbra-free-2099.zip

1. 下载解压

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/hfshaobing/picx-images-hosting@master/20251221/image.5zgip1iy5uk0.webp)

2. mac环境给可执行文件授权  

```sh
chmod 755 jetbra-free-darwin-amd64
```

3. 执行后自动在浏览器打开 127.0.0.1:8123

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/hfshaobing/picx-images-hosting@master/20251221/image.jsztu7prx00.webp)

4. 点击激活后填写激活信息重启idea即可
